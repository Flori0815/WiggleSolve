# High-lift and control surfaces

High-lift and control-surface kinematics are generated by parametric
functions in `core-js/src/mechanisms/`. Each generator returns an ordinary
[definition](definition-format.md), so the result can be simulated in the web
app, exported, edited or driven from tests. Generators model only the
kinematically required bodies and nodes; the geometry is attached as
[visual meshes](definition-format.md#visuals).

## Dropped-hinge flap

`droppedHingeFlap(params?)` — demo: *Aerospace → Dropped-Hinge Flap (swept,
tapered)* (`demos/dropped_hinge_flap.json`, regenerated with
`npm run generate:demos -w @wigglesolve/core`).

### Geometry

World axes: **x** aft, **y** up, **z** outboard. The hinge line is swept by
`hingeSweep` (default 25°). Sections are defined in the plane normal to the
hinge line and scaled linearly about the hinge point by the taper ratio
(`taper`, default 0.6 tip/root). The flap trailing edge is therefore swept as
well — with taper about the hinge line it is swept less than the hinge line
(≈ 18.5° with the defaults), as on a swept wing.

```
 support 1 (spanwise fixed)                support 2 (spanwise free)

 wing ──◉ GRA 1 (driven)                     wing ──◉ GRA 2 (driven)
        │ crank                                     │ crank
        ●━━━━ strut ━━━━● lug                       ●━━━━ strut ━━━━● lug
                 flap 1 │                                   flap 2 │
                 lever  │ (part of flap 1)          swivel ⊙       │ (flap-fixed axis)
                        ◎ spherical hinge            lever  │
                                                            ◎ spherical hinge

 flap 1 ═══════════════════ ⊕ ═══════════════════ flap 2
          split: revolute parallel to the hinge line, inside the
          flap section at 1/3 chord (1 DOF)
```

### Topology

| Body | Nodes | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `wing` | `hinge_1`, `hinge_2`, `gra_1_axis`, `gra_2_axis` | ground: spherical-joint centres and actuator axes |
| `crank_i` | `crank_i_tip` | rotary actuator output crank |
| `strut_i` | `strut_i_top`, `strut_i_end` | drive strut with rod ends |
| `flap_1` | `flap_1_lug`, `flap_split` | inboard panel incl. its hinge lever; carries the split axis |
| `flap_2` | `flap_2_lug`, `lever_2_pivot` | outboard panel |
| `lever_2` | `lever_2_end` | swivelling hinge lever of the spanwise-free support |

| Joint / actuator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `gra_1`, `gra_2` | the two driven rotary actuators (independent inputs) |
| `hinge_1_x/y/z` | spherical joint lever ↔ wing at the fixed support (three revolutes about wing-fixed axes) |
| `flap_split_rot` | the only relative freedom between the panels |
| `lever_2_swivel` | lets support 2 follow spanwise motion |
| `strut_i_rot`, `strut_i_tilt` | strut swing and rod-end tilt |

The spherical joint at the free support (`lever_2_end` ↔ `hinge_2`) is a
closure of the solving loop. The values of `hinge_1_x/y/z` are accumulated
rotations about fixed axes, not Euler angles, so their limits only bound
plausibility; the deflection is best read geometrically.

### Determinacy

Two panels and one lever have 18 DOF. The split revolute removes 5, the fixed
spherical joint 3, the swivel revolute 5, the free spherical joint 3 and the
two struts 1 each — leaving **0**. Both actuators are driven, and the system is not
over-determined thanks to the split axis:

- equal actuator positions rotate both panels about the hinge line, the split
  stays at 0°;
- any difference between the actuators (rigging, a lagging drive, even the
  full travel of one against the other) twists the panels against each other
  at the split and tilts the flap about the fixed spherical joint — because
  the split axis lies in the flap section, away from the hinge line (e.g.
  gra 1 at 60°, gra 2 at 0° → ≈ 49° at the split, ≈ 5–7° tilt);
- spanwise wing strain at support 2 is taken by the lever swivel;
- only a difference beyond `splitRange` jams; the solver then reports
  `flap_split_rot` at its limit.

### Solving

Because the split axis is offset from the hinge line, the three closures
(strut 1 ↔ lug 1, strut 2 ↔ lug 2, lever 2 ↔ hinge 2) are strongly coupled.
They are closed together by one `close_loops` step per iteration
(Levenberg-Marquardt over all nine joints); typically 3–5 iterations,
a few milliseconds.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `span`, `splitStation` | `[0, 3]`, `1.5` | extent and split, along the hinge line |
| `splitChordFraction` | `1/3` | split axis position in the section |
| `hingeSweep`, `taper` | `25°`, `0.6` | hinge-line sweep, tip/root chord ratio |
| `supports` | `0.75` fixed, `2.25` free | one support per panel, exactly one fixed |
| `hinge`, `lug`, `leverPivot` | `(0.60, −0.30)`, `(0.55, −0.15)`, `(0.60, −0.09)` | root section points |
| `profile` | 6-point section | flap section (index 0 = leading edge) |
| `actuatorAxis`, `crankLength`, `crankAngle` | `(0.15, −0.05)`, `0.15`, `0` | actuator and crank (root section) |
| `driveRange` | `0…60°` | travel of each rotary actuator |
| `flapRange`, `splitRange`, `swivelRange`, `sphericalRange`, `strutTilt` | `−70…10°`, `±60°`, `±15°`, `±45°`, `±10°` | joint limits |
| `tolerance`, `damping` | `1e-5`, `1e-3` | convergence tolerance, Levenberg-Marquardt λ |

With the defaults, 60° on both actuators deflects the flap by about 49°; the four-bar
transmission angle stays above 64°. All support sections are similar (scaled
about the hinge), so both supports command the same deflection.

### Verification

`core-js/tests/flaps.test.ts` checks hinge sweep, taper and trailing-edge
sweep, the split axis at 1/3 chord parallel to the hinge line, deflection
against the closed-form four-bar, independent drives over the full travel
(split takes the difference), a round trip back to the reference pose,
jamming beyond a narrowed split range, spanwise
offset taken by the swivel, the free support on either side and parameter
validation. `test-suite/py` runs the same demo through the Python port.

## Hinged surfaces

`hingedSurface(params)` builds any surface rotating about a (swept) hinge
line: spoiler, aileron, flaperon, slat or Krueger flap. Sections are given in
chord units relative to the hinge, normal to the hinge line, with the chord
varying linearly from root to tip. Two drives:

| Drive | Kinematics |
|---|---|
| `rotary` | the actuator turns the surface directly about the hinge; optional structure `fittings` and circular `tracks` (slats: the "hinge" is the virtual centre of the circular tracks) |
| `linear` | hydraulic cylinder pivoting on the structure at `mount`, rod attached to the surface `lug`; the stroke range is derived from the deflection range and checked for toggle positions; one `close_loops` loop closes rod ↔ lug |

`deployDown` reverses the positive sense (trailing edge or nose down).

## Widebody wing (787-class)

`widebodyWing(params?)` — demo: *Aerospace → Widebody Wing (787-class)*
(`demos/widebody_wing.json`). The right wing of a long-range twin-aisle
airliner, approximated from publicly quoted figures (not manufacturer data):

| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Side of body / trailing-edge kink / tip | 3 m / 10 m / 30 m from the centreline |
| Leading-edge sweep | 32.2° |
| Trailing-edge sweep inboard / outboard of the kink | 5° / 21° |
| Root chord, thickness | 10.5 m, 13 % → 9 % |

Surfaces and drives (groups give one master slider each in Simulate mode):

| Group | Surfaces | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Leading edge | Krueger flap inboard of the engine, 6 slats outboard | rotary: Krueger hinge; slats rotate about their circular-track centre |
| Flaps | inboard and outboard flap | `droppedHingeFlap` each (two supports, split panels, two driven rotary actuators) |
| Flaperon | flaperon behind the engine | linear actuator, −10° … +30° (down) |
| Aileron | outboard aileron | linear actuator, ±25° |
| Spoilers | 7 spoilers ahead of the flaps | linear actuator each, 0 … 60° |

Simplifications: one actuator per control surface and spoiler (real surfaces
often have several in active/active mode), no dihedral or twist, slat tracks
as exact circular arcs (rotation about their centre), fixed wing as a smooth
loft. The convergence tolerance is 0.1 mm, because positions ~25 m
from the origin carry ~1e-5 m of single-precision rounding.

### Composition

The wing is assembled from independent generators with
`prefixDefinition(def, prefix)` and `mergeDefinitions(name, description,
parts)` (`core-js/src/definition/compose.ts`), which rename every id and
reference and reject duplicates — the same approach works for any assembly of
mechanisms.
